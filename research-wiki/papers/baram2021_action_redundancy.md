# Action Redundancy in Reinforcement Learning

## One-line thesis
Transition entropy decomposes into model entropy plus action redundancy; in the deterministic case redundancy reduces to an action equivalence set, estimable near-model-free.

## Key Results
- The formal name for our measured utility-indifferent action sets (action redundancy/equivalence); estimation is precedented, but they minimize redundancy for exploration — nobody routes a secondary criterion through the sets.
