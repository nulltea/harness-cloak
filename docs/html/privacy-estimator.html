Privacy EstimatorSemantic-fact relation classification and formal universe count prediction

architecture report · 2026-08-03
Abstract

The v5 privacy estimator treats a source–candidate relation as one semantic fact observed through one or more clinical occurrence excerpts. A fully adapted ClinicalMosaic cross-encoder feeds four independently retrieved role slots to a hierarchical head: a symmetric connectedness decision and an ordered direction decision compose the required four-class probability distribution. This replaces the v4 single-query role pooling path and its top-four-layer-only adaptation. Training balances type, class, and semantic fact; checkpoint selection now averages admitted occurrence-view probabilities within each fact before scoring, so repeated views cannot dominate model selection. The detached count stage is unchanged: it fits a compact head over frozen candidate features from 19,385 ontology-defined universes.

Model Architecture

semantic facts · four-slot retrieval · hierarchical relation · detached count

Occurrence excerpts are evidence; semantic facts are the supervised and evaluated objects. The relation branch preserves source, candidate, occurrence, and global context as separate retrieved slots, then factors the label into the two decisions the ontology actually requires.

Relation path. The structured query contains typed source and candidate labels plus their canonical definitions; a second segment contains the authentic detected occurrence in its source excerpt. Thirteen atomic markers make these roles explicit. All twelve ClinicalMosaic layers adapt with layer-wise learning-rate decay. A two-layer, six-head Transformer decoder retrieves four 384-wide slots from every unmasked final token state, avoiding the irreversible compression of global masked-mean pooling and the single context-attention bottleneck of v4.

Structured decision and evaluation. A swap-invariant head predicts connected versus unrelated, while an ordered head predicts forward inclusion, reverse inclusion, or related without inclusion. Their probabilities compose valid generalization, reversal, related but non-substitutable, and unrelated. The exact composed four-class negative log likelihood trains the model. On development data, view probabilities are averaged once per semantic_relation_fact_id; fact-level macro-F1 and class recalls select the checkpoint. Row-weighted scores remain diagnostics only.

Count path. The count stage retains candidate-only serialization and masked-mean pooling because it predicts a property of one candidate rather than a directional relation. The relation-adapted encoder is frozen and cached; a 64-wide GELU head learns standardized log universe size with Smooth-L1 magnitude loss and pairwise narrow-to-broad ordering. Counts are formal ontology universe sizes, never corpus mention counts.
